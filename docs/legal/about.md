# About HealthAI Atlas

> **Canonical About-page copy** authored by Kate, 2026-04-29.
> This is the source of truth for the public site's `/about` page.

---

HealthAI Atlas is an independent project founded by Dr. Ekaterina Kondrateva to provide open, structured knowledge about the global healthcare AI ecosystem. The project exists to make ecosystem intelligence accessible to those who need it most: practicing clinicians integrating AI tools into care, students learning to evaluate medical AI critically, researchers seeking collaborators and context, and founders navigating an increasingly complex landscape.

The Atlas operates as a personal research and educational initiative, separate from any other professional affiliations or commercial ventures the founder is associated with. All public-facing content is freely accessible under an open-knowledge license, and the project welcomes contributions, corrections, and feedback from the global community.

To sustain ongoing development—including continuous data verification, geographic expansion, and methodology refinement—HealthAI Atlas offers professional tooling for institutional users: structured data exports, programmatic access via API, and custom analytical services. These commercial offerings serve enterprise users (investment firms, healthcare organizations, consultancies, government bodies) whose work benefits from decision-ready data infrastructure.

The principle guiding HealthAI Atlas: the knowledge stays open; the tooling for professional use sustains the work.

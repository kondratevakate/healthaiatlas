This is a **scope note**, not a survey. It states the boundary of the biosecurity
section before any records are entered, so that later additions can be judged
against a written rule rather than against taste.

## Why the Atlas covers biosecurity at all

HealthAI Atlas tracks where AI meets health systems. Biosecurity sits at that
intersection from two directions at once: AI methods are becoming inputs to
biological design, and biological risk is becoming an input to AI governance.
A directory that covers drug discovery and life-science tooling but omits the
safety layer describes half a system.

## In scope

- **Governance instruments** — the specific rules, executive actions, and
  international frameworks that bind biological work, and what each one actually
  requires of whom.
- **Screening and control points** — the places where a technical or procedural
  check exists between an intention and a physical outcome.
- **Institutional actors** — the bodies that write, enforce, or advise on the above.
- **Evaluation practice** — how biological capability and biological risk are
  measured, and what those measurements do and do not establish.

## Out of scope

- **Operational detail that functions as instruction.** The Atlas describes that
  a control exists, who administers it, and what it covers. It does not describe
  how to defeat it, and it does not aggregate technical specifics whose only
  use is circumvention. This is a hard boundary, not a matter of tone.
- **Threat speculation without an evidence trail.** Scenario writing is a real
  discipline, and it is not what this directory is. Entries describe things that
  have happened or rules that exist.
- **Clinical biosafety practice.** Laboratory safety at the bench level is a
  well-served field with its own institutions. The Atlas covers the governance
  layer above it.

## Evidence standard

Biosecurity is the section where the Atlas's general rule matters most: every
assertion links to primary evidence — the issuing body's own text, not a
summary of it. Where an entry rests on inference rather than a located source,
it says so in the entry itself.

## Status

This section is at scope-definition stage. Structured records are not yet
entered. Contributions that sharpen the boundary above are as useful right now
as contributions that add entries.

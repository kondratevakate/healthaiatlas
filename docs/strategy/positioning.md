# HealthAI Atlas — Positioning

> **HealthAI Atlas — open, structured knowledge about the healthcare AI ecosystem, with a professional tier for institutional users.**

## What HealthAI Atlas is

A research and educational project that maps the healthcare AI ecosystem — organizations, products, deployments, regulatory pathways, and the relationships between them. The public layer is open knowledge. The professional tier provides decision-ready data for institutional users (investment firms, healthcare organizations, consultancies, government bodies).

Operating principle:

> **The knowledge stays open; the tooling for professional use sustains the work.**

## Persona paths on the public site

The public site organizes navigation around four kinds of users:

- **Founders** — navigate the ecosystem, find collaborators and benchmarks
- **Job seekers** — see who is hiring in healthcare AI in the regions covered
- **Businesses** — evaluate vendors and partnership candidates
- **Clinicians** — understand AI tools in deployment and how to evaluate them critically

Each persona is meant to find a meaningful entry point on the public layer without needing the professional tier.

## Geographic coverage

The Atlas grows region by region. Currently active and "coming soon" regions are visible on the public ecosystem map; sign up for the waitlist on any "coming soon" region to be notified when its coverage ships.

The order in which regions are added is driven by where the founder has access to credible primary sources, not by addressable-market size alone — quality is non-negotiable.

## Why this exists

Healthcare AI ecosystems globally are fragmented. Generic databases (CB Insights, PitchBook, Rock Health) cover healthcare AI as a slice of larger products and lack clinical-deployment fidelity. Region-specific coverage with primary-source verification is what HealthAI Atlas provides.

The credibility of the project depends on data quality and methodology transparency — see [`docs/methodology.md`](../methodology.md) (forthcoming) for the verification pipeline and contribution rules.
